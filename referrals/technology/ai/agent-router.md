# Agent Router Referral Program

For each friend you invite to register, you get a **$150.00 reward** and your friend gets a **$50.00 reward** in AI credits (not real money).

### How It Works:
1. **Invite Friends**: Share your unique referral link with developers and AI builders.
2. **Friend Registers**: Your friend gets **$50.00 in AI credits** on signup.
3. **Receive $150.00 Reward**: You receive **$150.00 in AI credits** per registration.
4. **Transfer to Balance**: Transfer the reward amount to your account balance through the transfer function.
5. The more friends you invite, the more rewards you get.
