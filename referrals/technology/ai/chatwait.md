### What is Chatwait?
Chatwait is an open-source browser extension that displays one sponsored, dismissible line in the dead layout space beneath your prompt while AI models are generating text. Advertisers bid in a live auction for this placement, and Chatwait shares **50% of the revenue directly with you**.

### Key Features & Benefits:
- **50% Revenue Share**: Half of every dollar bid by advertisers goes directly to your balance.
- **Real USD Cash**: No convoluted reward tiers or points; withdraw real money via Whop directly to your bank account.
- **Privacy First (Zero Chat Access)**: The extension renders in layout dead space and never touches, reads, or transmits prompt or AI message content. Fully open-source on GitHub.
- **Cross-Platform AI Support**: Works seamlessly on ChatGPT (`chatgpt.com`), Claude (`claude.ai`), and Google Gemini (`gemini.google.com`).
- **No Clicking Required**: You earn simply for qualified impressions while the AI model is thinking.
