# Contributor Reputation System

Contributors earn verifiable on-chain/public reputation points and badges based on their community contributions.

## Point Allocations

- **Approved Referral Contribution**: `+10 points`
- **Accurate Community Verification Vote**: `+2 points`
- **Valid Expired / Stale Report**: `+5 points`
- **Valid Reward Change Report**: `+3 points`
- **High Quality Documentation Bonus**: `+2 points`
- **Spam or Misleading Submission Penalty**: `-25 to -100 points`

## Contributor Badges

- **Founding Contributor**: Early platform architect and maintainer
- **Verified Contributor**: 3+ approved referrals
- **Verification Expert**: 100+ accurate verification actions
- **Community Guardian**: 10+ validated triage reports
- **Deal Hunter**: First to spot and submit high-value promotions
